You choose how much notice you get for each payment. Orbra also adds everything up, so you can see your monthly and yearly total.

You add your bills. We never see your bank.

A lot of money apps ask you to connect your bank account. Orbra doesn’t. You add each subscription or bill yourself, with its price and renewal date, which takes about ten seconds each.

It means you decide exactly what’s in there, and you never hand over a bank login.

Setting it up

  1. Add what you pay for

    Pick a subscription or bill, then enter the price and the next date it comes out.

  2. Choose your reminders

    Decide how many days’ notice you want before each renewal.

  3. Get told in time

    Orbra reminds you before renewals, price rises and contract ends.
